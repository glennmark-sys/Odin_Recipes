Fried Chicken

Description

Fried chicken is a delicious dish made with chicken pieces coated in seasoned flour and fried until crispy and golden brown.

It is a popular comfort food that can be enjoyed with rice, gravy, or your favorite dipping sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 kilogram chicken pieces
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • Cooking oil

Steps

  1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper.
  2. Soak the chicken in buttermilk for at least 30 minutes.
  3. Mix flour, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper.
  4. Remove the chicken from the buttermilk.
  5. Coat each piece thoroughly with the seasoned flour.
  6. Heat cooking oil in a deep pan.
  7. Fry the chicken until crispy, golden brown, and fully cooked.
  8. Drain the chicken on a rack or paper towels.
  9. Serve hot.
