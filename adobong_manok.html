Adobong Manok

Description

Adobong Manok is a popular Filipino dish made with chicken simmered in soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and other seasonings.

It is commonly served with steamed rice and is known for its savory and tangy flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 kilogram chicken
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/3 cup vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 6 cloves garlic
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon sugar

Steps

  1. Heat cooking oil in a large pan.
  2. Sauté the garlic until lightly browned.
  3. Add the chicken and cook until lightly browned.
  4. Add soy sauce, vinegar, water, bay leaves, and black pepper.
  5. Bring the mixture to a boil.
  6. Lower the heat and simmer until the chicken is tender.
  7. Add sugar if desired and simmer for a few more minutes.
  8. Serve hot with steamed rice.
